Features

Built for the whole month, not just the receipt.

Tap through what Cashly does. Every preview uses the same pieces you will see in the app.

Stop finding out on the 30th.

Every budget has a pace bar: the fill is what you spent, the line is how much of the month has gone by. You see trouble while there is still time to fix it.

  • Budgets by category, for any month
  • Bills and expected income count in the plan from day one
  • Alerts at 80% and 100% of a budget, and a reminder to plan the next month

So fast you will actually keep doing it.

The amount comes first, big and ready to type. Pick a category and you are done: a coffee takes three taps.

  • Tags that cut across categories: Trip, Home, Work
  • Installments spread over the months automatically
  • Any currency, converted at the rate of the day

Answers, not just charts.

Cashly reads your history on your device and tells you what matters this month.

  • How much you can still spend per day without going over plan
  • Where the month will land, from your pace and the bills you recorded
  • Unusual spending, recurring charges and where you can save
  • A Cashly Summary written on your device by Apple Intelligence, on supported devices

  • Fifty levels, with a new title every five
  • Three short missions every week and one challenge a month
  • Streak freezes for the days life gets in the way

Record without opening the app.

Cashly lives where you already are on your iPhone.

  • Wallet capture: a Shortcuts automation sends every Apple Pay purchase to Cashly
  • Widgets on the Home Screen and Lock Screen
  • A New Expense control for Control Center and the Action button
  • Siri: "How much is left in Groceries in Cashly"

Shared costs without the awkward math.

Add the profiles that split the bill with you. Cashly works out each share and remembers who still owes what.

  • Equally, by percentage or by a fixed amount
  • Choose who paid, then mark each share as paid or to receive
  • Profiles for family, partners and roommates

Your money is nobody else's business.

There is no Cashly server holding your data and no bank password to hand over. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to leak.

  • Sync across your iPhone and iPad with your own iCloud
  • Export a backup or a spreadsheet anytime, free
  • Face ID lock and a discreet mode that blurs amounts

Questions

Before you download.

Do I need to connect my bank?

No. Cashly never asks for bank passwords. You record in a few taps, or let a Shortcuts automation capture Apple Pay purchases for you.

Where is my data stored?

On your devices and, with iCloud on, in your own private iCloud. There is no Cashly account or server. Read the Privacy Policy.

What is free and what needs Cashly Pro?

Recording, editing, budgets, the Journey, widgets, Siri, iCloud sync and export are free. Pro adds analyses and forecasts, on-device intelligence, automatic backups, imports, the Claude Code connection and exclusive icons and avatars.

What happens if I cancel Pro?

Everything you recorded stays readable, editable and exportable. Only the Pro features stop.

Which devices and languages?

iPhone and iPad today, with Mac and Apple Vision Pro coming soon. In English, Portuguese, Spanish, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Chinese and Russian, with any currency.
