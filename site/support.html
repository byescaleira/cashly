Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to us and include your device and Cashly version (Settings › About Cashly).

support@byescaleira.com

My data isn't syncing

Check that you are signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive on in every device, and that iCloud is on for Cashly. Settings › iCloud in Cashly shows the last sync.

How do I back up or move my data?

Settings › Data and Backup › Export Backup creates a file you can keep anywhere. Import it on any device from the same page.

How do I cancel Cashly Pro?

Open Settings › Cashly Pro in the app and tap Manage Subscription, or go to your Apple Account subscriptions. Your data stays readable and exportable after the subscription ends.

I bought something but don't see it

Tap Restore in the Cashly Pro screen or in the shop item. Purchases are tied to the Apple Account that made them.
